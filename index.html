About me.

I’m a Registered Social Worker and ABA therapist based in Scarborough, Ontario. At Surrey Place, I work one-to-one with children and youth aged 6 to 18 who have autism or developmental disabilities and complex or severe behavioural, communication and learning challenges. Before that, at Community Living Toronto, I supported adults with autism and developmental disabilities through individualized plans, life-skills programs and crisis support. My social work training in India and Canada shapes how I work: person-centred, equity-informed, and always alongside the family and care team.
